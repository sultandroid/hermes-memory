## ✅ Revised Insurance Requirements for AD Engineering (Design Consultants)

| Insurance Type | Minimum Limit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Professional Indemnity (PI)** | **SAR 5-10M** per claim & aggregate | Covers design errors and omissions; **primary protection** |
| **PI Period** | **7 years** after Final IFC acceptance | Latent defects emerge years later |
| **PI Retroactive Date** | **Date of this Agreement (or earlier)** | Covers design errors from inception |
| **PI Scope** | Must explicitly cover: design production, design review, IFC approval, stamping Samaya-drafted drawings (Mech), coordination with other specialists | Per verbal SOW |
| **Public Liability** | **SAR 1-2M** per occurrence | Occasional site visits; not full-time on site |
| **Workers' Compensation / Employer Liability** | Per Saudi Labor Law (lower estimate — 10-15 staff) | Office-based designers + site visits |
| **Cyber Liability** | **SAR 500k-1M** (recommended) | BIM files / project data |

### NOT Required (vs. Contractors)

| Insurance | Why not required |
|---|---|
| Construction All-Risk (CAR) | Designers don't construct |
| Plant & Equipment | Designers don't own heavy equipment |
| Erection All-Risk | Same |
| Third-Party Liability (high limits) | Designers present on site only occasionally |
| Decennial Liability (10-year structural) | Designers' liability is professional, not structural — covered by PI 7-year tail |

## 📋 Revised Insurance Clause (For Design Consultants)

```
ARTICLE 9.X — INSURANCE

X.1 The Second Party shall, at its own cost, procure and maintain throughout
    the duration of this Agreement and for a period of seven (7) years after
    Final IFC acceptance, the following insurances with insurers licensed
    in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia:

    (a) Professional Indemnity Insurance with a limit of not less than
        SAR [5,000,000] per claim and in aggregate, covering:

        (i) Design errors, omissions, and professional negligence
            arising from the Services performed under this Agreement;
        (ii) Design review of drawings prepared by the First Party
             (Mechanical Discipline);
        (iii) IFC approval and stamping of deliverables;
        (iv) Coordination with Studio ZNA, Rawasin, BMS Specialist;
        (v) Authority submissions (Civil Defense, Municipality, utilities)
            within the agreed Services;

        with a retroactive date not later than the date of this Agreement
        and a discovery period of seven (7) years after Final IFC acceptance;

    (b) Public Liability Insurance with a limit of not less than
        SAR [1,000,000] per occurrence, covering bodily injury and
        property damage arising from site visits;

    (c) Workers' Compensation / Employer Liability Insurance covering
        all personnel of the Second Party in compliance with Saudi
        Labor Law.

X.2 The Second Party shall deliver to the First Party a Certificate of
    Insurance evidencing the above coverages before the commencement of
    Services (Notice to Proceed).

X.3 The Second Party shall provide evidence of renewal of each policy
    not less than thirty (30) days before expiry.

X.4 The First Party shall be named as an additional insured under
    the Public Liability policy.

X.5 Failure to maintain the required insurances shall constitute a
    material breach of this Agreement, entitling the First Party to
    suspend Services and/or terminate under Art. 10.
```

## 📚 Lessons to Save (Design vs Contractor Insurance)

1. **"Design consultants ≠ contractors"** — insurance profile is fundamentally different
2. **"PI Insurance is the primary protection for designers"** — not Public Liability
3. **"PI 7-year tail is industry standard"** — covers latent defects in design
4. **"PI retroactive date = contract date"** — covers prior work affecting current project
5. **"Workers' Comp for designers = smaller estimate"** — office-based, occasional site visits
6. **"Public Liability for designers = lower limits"** — SAR 1-2M vs SAR 5-10M for contractors
7. **"Cyber Liability is recommended for designers"** — BIM / project data exposure
8. **"Always verify PI scope matches the actual SOW"** — design review, stamping, IFC, coordination must all be covered
