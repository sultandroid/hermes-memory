## 💡 New Risks from the Verbal SOW

### Risk 1: **Split Mechanical responsibility creates ambiguity**

| Scenario | Samaya draws | AD Engineering approves |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanical drawing has error | Samaya's drafter? Or AD Engineering's reviewer? | **Both could argue the other is responsible** |

**Critical:** The contract currently has no clause allocating **drawing errors vs approval errors**. If Samaya's drafter makes a mistake that AD Engineering's reviewer misses, **who pays?**

**Recommendation:** Add a "design chain liability" clause:
> *"Where the Mechanical drawings are prepared by the First Party and approved by the Second Party, errors shall be allocated as follows: (a) Drawing errors not reasonably discoverable by a competent reviewer = First Party's responsibility; (b) Errors that should have been caught during review = Second Party's responsibility. Errors in either case shall be corrected at no additional cost."*

### Risk 2: **AD Engineering becomes bottleneck if Mechanical review lags**

If Samaya drafts Mechanical drawings in week 1, AD Engineering has 1-2 weeks to review. If AD Engineering is busy with Electrical production, **Mechanical review slips → cascading delay**.

**Recommendation:** Add parallel-tracking clause:
> *"The Second Party shall ensure that Mechanical review capacity is reserved separately from Electrical production. Mechanical review shall not be delayed by Electrical work."*

### Risk 3: **Shop Drawing Reviews create new payment trigger**

The Tables 0 + 1 only cover design phases. Shop Drawing Reviews during construction = **NEW SCOPE** not in the SAR 225,000 contract price.

**Recommendation:** Add shop drawing review fees:
> *"Shop drawing reviews shall be compensated at the Second Party's prevailing professional rates (estimated [X] submissions per discipline), capped at [Y%] of the Contract Price. Any reviews above the cap shall be treated as variations under Art. 7."*

Or: include a fixed retainer for shop drawing reviews (e.g., SAR 25,000) within the contract price.

### Risk 4: **"AD Engineering is responsible toward CG" — does this mean they sign/stamp?**

| Item | Stamp? |
|---|---|
| Mechanical drawings (drafted by Samaya) | **AD Engineering stamp** = responsibility shift |
| Electrical drawings (drafted by AD Engineering) | **AD Engineering stamp** = normal |
| IFC approval letter | **AD Engineering stamp** = responsible signatory |

**Critical:** AD Engineering's stamp on Mechanical drawings (drafted by Samaya) = **professional responsibility for work they didn't draw**. **AD Engineering may resist this** — it's a heavy professional liability.

**The Saudi Council of Engineers and insurance providers may have views on stamping work not personally drawn.** Need to verify before signing.

**Recommendation:** Confirm with AD Engineering's PI insurer that stamping Samaya-drafted drawings is covered.

SCHEDULE A — DETAILED SCOPE OF SERVICES (revised per verbal agreement 2026-07-31)

A.1 MECHANICAL DISCIPLINE — SPLIT RESPONSIBILITY

  A.1.1 First Party (Samaya) shall:
    (a) Prepare Mechanical drawings using its in-house drafting team
    (b) Submit Mechanical drawings to the Second Party for review
    (c) Coordinate Mechanical design with the Architect (NRS) at the BIM level

  A.1.2 Second Party (AD Engineering) shall:
    (a) Review Mechanical drawings within 10 Working Days of receipt
    (b) Issue review comments in writing
    (c) Formally approve Mechanical drawings by stamping and signing
    (d) Assume Engineer of Record responsibility toward the Consultant
    (e) Submit approved Mechanical drawings to the Consultant (CG)

  A.1.3 Liability allocation:
    (a) Drawing errors reasonably discoverable by a competent reviewer =
        Second Party's responsibility (review failure)
    (b) Errors not reasonably discoverable = First Party's responsibility
    (c) Both parties shall cooperate to correct errors at no additional cost

A.2 ELECTRICAL DISCIPLINE — FULL AD ENGINEERING RESPONSIBILITY

  A.2.1 Second Party (AD Engineering) shall:
    (a) Produce all Electrical drawings (single-line, layout, load calcs)
    (b) Produce Electrical specifications and BOQ
    (c) Formally approve and stamp all Electrical deliverables
    (d) Assume full Engineer of Record responsibility toward the Consultant
    (e) Submit approved Electrical deliverables to the Consultant

A.3 BIM COORDINATION — SAMAYA'S RESPONSIBILITY

  A.3.1 First Party (Samaya) shall:
    (a) Manage BIM coordination across all disciplines
    (b) Operate the federated BIM model
    (c) Conduct weekly multi-discipline BIM coordination meetings
    (d) Issue clash reports to all specialists

  A.3.2 Second Party (AD Engineering) shall:
    (a) Provide clash detection input within Mechanical + Electrical disciplines
    (b) Resolve clashes within own disciplines within 5 Working Days
    (c) Coordinate with the First Party's BIM team as required

A.4 CROSS-DISCIPLINE COORDINATION — AD ENGINEERING (targeted)

       coordination meetings with these specialists as required,

A.5 IFC REVIEW AND APPROVAL — 100% AD ENGINEERING

    (a) Review 100% of the IFC package (Mechanical + Electrical)
    (b) Verify compliance with applicable codes (SBC, NFPA, etc.)

    (d) Submit the IFC package to the Consultant (CG)

    (d) Estimated volume: [X] Mechanical + [Y] Electrical submissions
    (e) Compensation: included in Contract Price up to [Z] submissions;
        above that, treated as variations under Art. 7
