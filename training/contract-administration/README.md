# Contract Administration (إدارة العقود)

> **Status:** Scaffolded (no cases yet)
> **Related topics:** claims-arbitration, qs-cost-control

## Purpose

Variation orders, EOT claims, contract clauses, scope creep, contractual notices, time-bar provisions.

## Scope

Everything between contract signature and final completion. Includes drafting and defending contractual correspondence, VOs, EOTs, claims, and notices.

## Why this matters

Most project failures are contract administration failures, not engineering failures. The difference between a profitable project and a loss is often a single missing notice.
