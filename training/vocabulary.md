# Cross-Topic Vocabulary — Arabic ↔ English

> **General construction/contract terms used across multiple training topics.** Topic-specific vocabulary lives in each topic's `vocabulary.md`.
>
> **Legal weight legend:**
> - ✅ **Strong** — survives arbitration/court review, has precedent
> - ⚠️ **Defensible** — depends on framing and evidence
> - ❌ **Weak** — frequently struck, hard to win on
