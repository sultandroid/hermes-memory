## Theme Coverage Notes

- **Theme 1 (PQ / tender disputes)**: Case 003 (DIT v APD ICC arbitration, Cameroon container terminal, November 2020) is the lead case. The case establishes that *wrongful exclusion from a tender* is arbitratable, not just judicially reviewable, and that the arbitral remedy can include re-issuance of the tender notice.
- **Theme 4 (subcontractor back-charges)**: four cases (002, 004, 007, 008) cover the contractual framework (002 — back-to-back in Abu Dhabi), the empirical proof (004 — USD 658,000 / 16 items airport case study), the common-law doctrinal anchor (007 — *Impact Painting v Man-Shield* four-stage test), and the FIDIC procedural workflow (008 — six-clause gateway under Cl. 7.5/7.6/11.4/15.1/2.5/3.5).
- **Theme 6 (bond forfeiture)**: three cases (001, 002, 005). Case 001 is the *defence* (DIFC bond restraint, fraud / unconscionability test). Case 002 is the *contractual frame* (back-to-back bonds and payment flow-down). Case 005 is the *offence* (URDG 758, strict-compliance test, *Tecnicas Reunidas* standalone code).
- **Theme 7 (FIDIC Cl. 16)**: two cases (006, 008). Case 006 is the Howard Kennedy practitioner commentary on Cl. 16.1–16.4 (suspension, termination, payment-on-termination). Case 008 is the FIDIC Employer Claims / IPC-deduction procedural cluster that *triggers* the Cl. 16.1 / 16.2(c) remedies.
