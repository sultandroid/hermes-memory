# Design Management — Top Lessons

> **Curated wisdom** extracted from the 9 cases built on 31 July 2026. Read first; jump to `cases/INDEX.md` for the per-case detail.

## From Case 001 (RIBA Plan of Work) — Stage Discipline

1. **RIBA 2020 Plan of Work has 8 stages (0-7) with explicit Information Requirement drops at each stage gate.**
   The Saudi National BIM Strategy explicitly aligns with RIBA 2020. **Operational rule:** every design submission must identify the RIBA stage and the Information Requirement at that stage; otherwise it's an unmeasurable submission.

2. **Stage 4 (Technical Design) is the linchpin stage for Saudi construction.**
   It is the last stage at which design changes are "free" — pre-Stage 5 (Manufacturing & Construction), a design change costs design time only. Post-Stage 5, the same change costs construction time, materials, and rework. **Practical consequence:** invest in Stage 4 review. The cost of getting Stage 4 right is 100x less than the cost of fixing Stage 5 issues on site.

## From Cases 002 + 003 + 005 + 008 (Design change control) — The Most-Disputed Area

3. **HKA CRUX 2026 finding: late or incomplete design information is the #1 driver of claims in Saudi construction.**
   The four main causes: (a) Employer brief changes mid-design, (b) inadequate site investigation at Stage 2, (c) late specialist input (MEP, structural, fire), (d) Stage 4 "value engineering" that re-opens settled decisions. **Strategic consequence:** the contractor's first move in any claim is to demonstrate that the design information was late, not that the contractor was slow.

4. **A design change is a Variation only if it changes scope, not if it changes the means of execution.**
   "Use a different brand of cement" is not a Variation. "Change the building height by 2m" is. **Drafting consequence:** every RFIs / Design Query should be classified by both parties (Variation / No Variation) at submission, not at claim time.

5. **The VE (Value Engineering) rejection rate in Saudi construction is ~50% (MDPI Buildings 2024, Vol. 14, Art. 1017).**
   The most common rejection reason: the proposed VE saves CapEx but increases OpEx or maintenance. **Operational rule:** always quantify the whole-life cost impact of a VE proposal, not just the initial saving.

## From Case 004 (As-built drawings) — Closeout

6. **As-built drawings are a contract document, not a contractor courtesy.**
   Most FIDIC contracts require the Contractor to provide as-builts before the final certificate. Incomplete as-builts are a ground for withholding the final payment. **Operational rule:** maintain as-builts continuously, not as a closeout scramble.

7. **The "red-line" master on site is the source of truth for the as-built set.**
   Every change on site (RFIs, site instructions, substitutions) must be reflected on the red-line master within 7 days. The BIM model and the as-built drawings are downstream of the red-line master.

## From Case 006 (CII Constructability) — Pre-Construction Review

8. **CII RT-034: a Constructability *Programme* (not a one-off review) is the standard for mega-projects.**
   The Programme runs from Stage 3 (Spatial Coordination) through Stage 5 (Manufacturing & Construction), with monthly reviews, a defined Constructability Manager, and a clear sign-off authority. **Operational rule:** for any project > SAR 100M, propose a Constructability Programme in the tender — not a single review.

## From Case 007 (BIM Coordination — Saudi National Strategy) — Multi-Discipline

9. **The Saudi National BIM Strategy (2024) mandates ISO 19650 compliance on Vision 2030 mega-projects.**
   Non-compliance is increasingly a *contractual* defect, not just a technical one. **Operational rule:** your BEP must reference ISO 19650 explicitly; your CDE workflow must follow ISO 19650-2.

## From Case 009 (EPC Design Freeze) — The Hidden 70%

10. **70% of EPC disputes are locked in by the Engineering Phase (per industry analyst Syed, 2025).**
    The single biggest EPC lesson: **do not start procurement before Engineering is frozen**. The cost of waiting 3 months for engineering completion is always less than the cost of re-procurement after a design change.

11. **The Design Freeze Certificate (DFC) is the contractual milestone that locks engineering.**
    Post-DFC, any design change is a Variation. Pre-DFC, design is iterative. **Strategic rule:** push for the DFC as early as possible; every design change after DFC is your Variation.

## Cross-cutting

12. **Design management is the most under-resourced function in Saudi construction, and the highest-leverage place to invest.**
    A 1% improvement in design information completeness saves 5% in construction cost (industry rule of thumb, per HKA CRUX). The design manager is the most underpaid person on the project for the value they create.
