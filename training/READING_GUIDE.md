# Reading Guide — How to Study a Saudi Commercial Arbitration Case

> **Use this guide every time the user pastes a case PDF.** The goal is to extract 5 lessons, not just summarize.

## The 5-Minute Scan

Read the operative clauses (منطوق الحكم) first, not the reasoning. The operative clauses tell you:
- Who won
- How much
- Which heads were struck, which were upheld
- The court's exact wording (which is the legal precedent)

The reasoning matters for the **why** — but the operative clauses are the precedent.

## The 30-Minute Deep Read

Then read in this order:

1. **Parties + counsel** — who's who, who's the technical expert, who's the arbitrator
2. **Procedural history** — when was the award issued, when was nullity filed, was it within the 30-day window (Art. 50/2)
3. **The award's 7 monetary heads** — extract each into a table
4. **The tribunal's reasoning** — what did it accept, what did it reject, and the **exact Arabic wording** for each
5. **The court's grounds for nullity** — listed in the petition (usually 4–6 grounds)
6. **The court's analysis per ground** — table form: ground raised | court's finding | reasoning
7. **The operative clauses** — verbatim translation
8. **Lessons** — see below

## The 5-Lesson Extraction

For every case, extract 5 lessons. Force yourself to find:

1. **The vocabulary lesson** — which exact Arabic phrase made the difference
2. **The evidence lesson** — what document was the linchpin
3. **The procedural lesson** — what deadline or step was decisive
4. **The strategic lesson** — what would the loser have done differently
5. **The principle** — the one sentence the court articulated that has wider application

If you can't find all 5, the case is interesting but not yet a lesson. Re-read.

## The Red Flags (warnings to remember)

When you see these patterns in a case, **save them**:

- 🚩 "فوائد ربوية" mentioned in the operative clauses — ribā case
- 🚩 "إكراه" or "تدليس" alleged — fraud/duress claim
- 🚩 "عدم اختصاص" — jurisdictional challenge (often fatal)
- 🚩 "مخالفة النظام العام" — public policy violation (very high bar to win)
- 🚩 "تعارض مع حكم قضائي نهائي" — conflict with a final judicial ruling
- 🚩 Award issued **after** the contractual deadline — may be annulled

## The Green Flags (signs of a strong claim)

- ✅ "تكاليف فعلية" — actual costs
- ✅ "تعويض عن ضرر حقيقي" — real damage
- ✅ "إرباك المشروع" — project disruption framing
- ✅ Bank statements attached
- ✅ Site instructions / variation orders attached
- ✅ Initial delivery certificate present
- ✅ Delay analysis in critical path method (CPM)
- ✅ Contemporaneous records (daily reports, minutes)

## When in Doubt

Ask: *"What would the loser have done differently if they had to do this case over?"*

That answer is the lesson.
