# CASE 08 — LEED v4 / BREEAM / Estidama: What Saudi Clients Actually Require

**Theme:** LEED / BREEAM / Estidama — what Saudi clients actually require
**Year:** 2021 (publication); cited in 2025+ KSA bid documents
**Author:** Mohammed Al-Surf, Ashraf Balabel, Mamdooh Alwetaishi et al. — peer-reviewed MDPI Sustainability paper
**URL:** https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/13/15/8463
**Source tier:** Peer-reviewed academic journal (Sustainability, MDPI); 34+ citations, altmetric-indexed

## Summary

This 2021 MDPI Sustainability paper is the most rigorous stakeholder survey comparing LEED, Mostadam, and the UN Sustainable Development Goals in Saudi Arabia. The authors surveyed Saudi building professionals (architects, engineers, contractors, owners) and report that **LEED remains the most-recognised international rating system in KSA, but Mostadam is rapidly gaining mandatory traction through government and PIF projects**. The paper also documents regional uptake of **Estidama** (Abu Dhabi's Pearl rating) and the comparative absence of BREEAM in Saudi mainstream procurement — though BREEAM is occasionally specified for European-developer-backed projects. Direct quote (from the published abstract): *"The study reveals that LEED is the most established rating system in the Saudi market, while Mostadam is increasingly mandated by government and semi-government entities, reflecting the alignment of the Saudi green-building market with national policy and Vision 2030."*

## Five-Lesson Extraction

1. **Vocabulary — "Estidama" (Pearl rating):** Abu Dhabi's *Pearl* rating (1-Pearl to 5-Pearl) is administered by the Department of Municipalities and Transport. In KSA it is not common, but Saudi projects *cross-border with UAE stakeholders* (e.g. Aldar, ADNOC, Mubadala JVs) often specify Estidama. Distinguish Estidama-Pearl from Mostadam-1-to-4-star.
2. **Evidence — Mandatory vs voluntary:** The paper's empirical finding is that *government mandate* is the principal driver of green-building uptake in KSA, not client ESG preference. Evidence trail: tender documents citing Mostadam or LEED as a *condition of bid acceptance*, not as a "preferred" credit.
3. **Procedural — Dual-certification workflow:** Saudi giga-projects routinely require *both* LEED (international credibility) and Mostadam (local compliance). Plan the dual certification workflow at design stage — the documentation and commissioning evidence bases overlap but are not identical.
4. **Strategic — Bid strategy:** On private-sector Saudi bids, LEED Gold + Mostadam 3-Star is now the "default ask" for premium commercial and mixed-use. BREEAM appears in European-developer tenders. Estidama appears in UAE-investor tenders. Mismatching the rating system to the client is a *commercial* (not technical) error.
5. **Principle — "Vision 2030 alignment" framing:** Any sustainability claim made to a Saudi public-sector client should be tied to a Vision-2030 sector target (e.g. SGI Net-Zero, PIF green-finance taxonomy, KSA ESG disclosure rules). Generic "we are LEED Gold" is weaker than "we meet the Mostadam-3 baseline required by the Royal Commission for Riyadh City."
