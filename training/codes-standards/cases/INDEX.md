## Key Findings (SBC 2024 update)

- **SBC 2024 mandatory from 30 June 2025** after 180-day transition from M/88/2024
- **New codes added:** Seismic Design for Steel, Repair/Rehabilitation of Existing Concrete, GFRP-Reinforced Concrete
- **Administered by Saudi Building Code Center under MOMAH** (since 4 March 2025)
- **Article 9 (amended 9 Oct 2024):** occupancy certificate now prerequisite for electrical activation — the real teeth in defect-liability claims
