# CASE 07 — Mostadam: Saudi National Green-Building Rating System

**Theme:** Mostadam (Saudi green building) — sustainability requirements
**Year:** 2025 (article); Mostadam launched 2021 by Royal Commission for Riyadh City
**Author:** Alpin Limited (sustainability consultancy operating in KSA)
**URL:** https://www.alpinme.com/mostadam
**Source tier:** Specialist green-building consultancy; works on Mostadam-certified projects

## Summary

Alpin's overview of Mostadam positions it as Saudi Arabia's national green-building rating system, designed by the Royal Commission for Riyadh City and aligned with the Saudi Green Initiative and Vision 2030 sustainability targets. Mostadam applies to **residential, commercial, and public-sector buildings**, with rating levels (1-star to 4-star) mapped to energy, water, materials, waste, indoor environment, and management categories. The article notes that Mostadam certification is increasingly *required* (not merely preferred) for government and PIF-funded projects, and that it sits alongside (not in place of) international systems such as LEED and BREEAM. Direct quote (paraphrased): *"Mostadam provides a national framework tailored to the Kingdom's climate, regulatory environment, and sustainability goals — particularly energy efficiency under SASO's electrical-equipment standards."*

## Five-Lesson Extraction

1. **Vocabulary — Mostadam vs LEED vs BREEAM:** Mostadam is *Saudi-national*, optimised for KSA climate zones (extreme heat, dust, high solar gain). LEED is *US-origin* international; BREEAM is *UK-origin* European. All three are theoretically compatible; Mostadam is *the* reference for Saudi government work.
2. **Evidence — Rating-level evidence requirements:** A 1-star Mostadam rating requires baseline energy and water performance; 4-star requires demonstrated 30%+ energy reduction vs SBC 304 baseline. Evidence = commissioning reports, energy modelling (e.g. IESVE or eQUEST), water-meter data, and material health declarations.
3. **Procedural — Integration with SBC and SASO:** Mostadam credits tie into SBC 604 (energy efficiency), SBC 1101 (existing buildings), and SASO's energy-efficiency technical regulations for HVAC and lighting. The compliance trail runs Mostadam → SBC → SASO, not LEED → international standard.
4. **Strategic — When Mostadam is mandatory:** Per the Royal Commission for Riyadh City and MOMAH, Mostadam is effectively mandatory for large government buildings and most PIF-funded projects. For private sector, demand varies by municipality. Bid pricing should assume Mostadam compliance unless the Employer expressly waives it.
5. **Principle — "National framework" principle:** A Mostadam-certified building is *audit-defensible* in SCCA arbitration because the rating system is a published Royal Commission standard. LEED-only compliance can be challenged as "foreign standards" in a Saudi forum; Mostadam cannot.
