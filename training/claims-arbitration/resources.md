# Claims & Arbitration — Resources

## Saudi-Specific

- **نظام التحكيم السعودي** (Saudi Arbitration Law) — Royal Decree M/34, 24/5/1433 H
- **اللائحة التنفيذية لنظام التحكيم** (Implementing Regulations)
- **اتفاقية الرياض العربية 1983** (Riyadh Arab Agreement for Judicial Cooperation)
- **Ministry of Justice** circulars on arbitration enforcement
- **Saudi Center for Commercial Arbitration (SCCA)** — institution rules and model clauses

## International

- **UNCITRAL Model Law** (basis for many national laws)
- **ICC Arbitration Rules**
- **LCIA Rules**
- **DIAC Rules** (Dubai)
- **QICCA Rules** (Qatar)
- **New York Convention 1958** (recognition and enforcement)

## Books

- **Redfern & Hunter on International Arbitration** (Oxford) — foundational
- **Born's International Commercial Arbitration** (3rd ed.) — comprehensive
- **Saudi Arbitration Law: Cases and Commentary** (Arabic)
- **The Construction Arbitration Handbook** (Haidar)
