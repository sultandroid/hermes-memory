# Claims & Arbitration — Top Lessons

> **Curated wisdom** extracted from cases in `cases/`. Each lesson is the takeaway the user should remember without re-reading the case.
>
> **Order:** most important first. Add new lessons here as cases are processed; the 5-lesson extraction method is in `../READING_GUIDE.md`.

## From Case 001 (Binladin vs Munshaat 2022) — Foundation

1. **Vocabulary discipline is the difference between winning and losing.**
   The contractor's bank-financing interest survived nullity because it was framed as "تعويض عن تكاليف فعلية" (actual costs), not "فوائد تأخير" (late-payment interest). The same riyal, different framing, different outcome.

2. **"إرباك المشروع" (project disruption) is the winning concept for delay-related claims.**
   It is stronger than "lost profits" (أرباح فاتتة), "inflation" (تضخم), or "delay damages" (تعويضات تأخير). The contractor won 61.1M SAR in indirect admin costs because of this framing.

3. **The initial delivery certificate (شهادة استلام ابتدائي) is the single most valuable document.**
   It locks the timeline (date 14/02/1437 H anchored the 891-day extension), anchors the contract balance, and makes the 9.5M initial delivery value claim solid. Protect it; verify the date; don't let it sit unsigned.

4. **Additional arbitration fees will get struck.**
   Under Art. "عاشراً" of typical Saudi arbitration deeds, extra tribunal fees are capped to *new requests by a party* — not "increased workload" or "longer proceedings." The court enforced this and ordered the SAR 902,558 returned.

5. **Document the rejection of a technical expert explicitly in the operative clauses.**
   A tribunal may reject a technical expert's opinion and substitute its own reading, but the reasoning must be articulated in the award. A silent rejection is a ground for nullity.

## From Case 005 (Riyadh 7 Oct 2024, 4630279062) — THE Art. 24/عاشراً Cap Case

6. **"Determining its own fees" is the prohibited act. "Allocating fees the parties already agreed to" is permissible.**
   If the arbitration deed is silent on fees, the Saudi route is **the competent court under Article 24 SAL** — not the tribunal. The tribunal has no authority to set fees in absence of agreement; silence = jurisdiction lacking.

7. **Saudi courts invoke Sharia sua sponte on the fee issue.**
   Even where the respondent agrees the fees were wrong, the court will annul because Art. 50(2) lets the court act on the Sharia ground on its own initiative. You cannot settle a Sharia-nullity ground by consent.

8. **Article 50(1)(f) is the operative ground for "ruled on matters not submitted."**
   When the tribunal goes beyond what the parties asked for — including on its own fees — Art. 50(1)(f) is invoked. The court will annul, then route the fee question back to the competent court.

## From Case 003 (Jeddah 4 Oct 2022, 4430103807) — The إرباك المشروع Seed Case

9. **The published Saudi Arabic terminology is "تكاليف زمنية" / "مصاريف إدارية غير مباشرة", not "إرباك المشروع".**
   Same concept, different label. Use the published terminology in court filings; reserve "إرباك المشروع" for your training notes.

10. **Tie the claim to documented delay, not to the running of the clock.**
    871 days of documented delay = "time-related costs" (upheld). Penalty for the running of the clock = "late payment penalty" (struck as ribā, see Case 004). The evidentiary hook matters.

## From Case 002 (Jeddah 22 June 2025, 4631092403) — The Textbook Partial Nullity

11. **Partial nullity operates on operative paragraphs, not on the whole award.**
    The court annulled *only* the paragraph that declared the contract null, while *upholding* the dismissal of other claims, the order that one party pay SAR 121,667, and the dismissal of remaining requests. The rest of the award is enforceable. Read the operative paragraphs one by one; never assume a successful nullity petition kills the whole award.

## From Case 010 (Riyadh 8 Aug 2022, 447044096) — Lost Profits Are Not Per Se Ribā

12. **Lost profits survive Sharia review if the methodology is sound.**
    Article 137 of the 2023 Civil Transactions Law now codifies this. If you can show the lost-profit calculation is tied to specific lost opportunities (not a flat % of contract value), the court will entertain it.

## From Case 004 (Jeddah 5 Dec 2018, 1491/1439H) — The Negative Example

13. **A "late penalty compensation" framed as a debt-increment will be struck.**
    Contrast with Case 003: the same court in the same period will enforce time-related costs (tied to delay) and strike late penalties (tied to the clock). The framing is the entire game.

## From Case 008 (Riyadh 22 Oct 2023, 4530340196) — Ejar / Public Policy

14. **Unregistered leases (Ejar) will not be enforced.**
    On public-policy grounds, an award that treats an unregistered lease as valid is annulled. **Drafting consequence:** any claim that depends on the validity of an unregistered arrangement is a ground for nullity, even if the substantive dispute is unrelated.

## From Case 011 (Riyadh 2017-2021) — Impartiality

15. **Counsel/arbitrator overlap = ground for nullity.**
    If any member of the tribunal has previously acted as counsel for one of the parties (in the same matter or a related one), the award is vulnerable to an impartiality challenge under Art. 50(1)(a).

## From Case 012 (Eastern Province 2017-2021) — Award Must End the Dispute

16. **An award that fails to dispose of all claims is a nullity.**
    Under Art. 40(1) and 41(1) SAL, the award must end the dispute. If the tribunal leaves an issue unaddressed, the award is incomplete and vulnerable to annulment. **Always check the operative paragraphs for completeness before issuing.**
