# Claims & Arbitration Vocabulary — Arabic ↔ English

> **Topic-specific terms.** General construction terms are in `../vocabulary.md` (cross-topic). Terms here are specific to Claims & Arbitration.

|---|---|---|
| Nullity petition (against award) | دعوى بطلان حكم تحكيم | ✅ Strong — 30-day window per Art. 50/2 |
| Arbitration award | حكم تحكيم | ✅✅ Critical document |
| Partial nullity | بطلان جزئي | ⚠️ Defensible — usually only the "fees" paragraph |
| Dismissal of nullity | رفض دعوى البطلان | ✅ The win |
| Settlement | صلح / تسوية | ✅ Strong — usually cheaper than tribunal |
| DAB (Dispute Adjudication Board) | مجلس فض النزاعات | ✅ Strong if per FIDIC |
| Without prejudice | بدون إجحاف | ✅ Strong — protects negotiations |
| Operative clauses (of judgment) | منطوق الحكم | ✅✅ Read first |
| Reasoning of judgment | أسباب الحكم | ✅ Strong — read second |
| Article 50 grounds | أسباب المادة 50 | ✅ Strong |
| Art. 50/2 (time limit) | المادة 50/2 (مدة 30 يوم) | ✅✅ Critical — strict |
| Art. 52/4 (governing rules) | المادة 52/4 (القواعد الحاكمة) | ✅ Strong |
| Art. 68 (variation cost) | المادة 68 (تكلفة التغيير) | ✅ Strong |
