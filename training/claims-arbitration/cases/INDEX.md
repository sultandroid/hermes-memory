## The Headline Stats (SCCA 2026 Country Report)

From the SCCA 2026 Country Report (1 July 2026), cited across 5+ law-firm digests:
- 967 Saudi Court of Appeal decisions reviewed (Jan 2023 – June 2025)
- 194 annulment applications (20.1% of total)
- 174 rejected (89.7% rejection rate)
- 20 succeeded: 12 full annulment (6.2%) + 8 partial (4.1%)
- **Only 1/194 (0.5%) succeeded on a Sharia ground — that case is Case 005 above**

## What was NOT found

- A direct, case-specific use of the Arabic term **"إرباك المشروع"** as a labelled legal concept in any published Saudi judgment. The SCCA English summary uses "time-related costs / indirect administrative expenses" for the same concept (Case 003). **Drafting recommendation:** when citing Case 003 to a Saudi court, use the Arabic term **"تكاليف زمنية" / "مصاريف إدارية غير مباشرة"**. Reserve "إرباك المشروع" as the working shorthand in your training notes.
- Full Arabic PDFs for 10 of the 11 cases. The SADR portal's public judgments index returned 404 for the indexing keys tested on cases 003-012. Only Case 002 (Jeddah 4631092403, June 2025) returned a full 33-page English translation directly.
- Saudi-specific case law on "without prejudice" settlement offers is thin on the public web. See `../negotiation-disputes/` for the closest equivalents (Dubai Court of Cassation Cases 31/2024 and 486/2024).
