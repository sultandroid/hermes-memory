## Summary
Practitioner-led application of Fisher & Ury's four principles — separate the people from the problem; focus on interests not positions; invent options for mutual gain; insist on objective criteria — to live construction disputes. Uses the 2023 Arcadis Global Construction Disputes Report as the BATNA benchmark (a 42 % rise to US$42.8 M in the average value of North American disputes in 2022, and a 13.6-month average resolution time). Sets out worked examples for "heated exchanges" between owner and contractor over workmanship, drawing on the author's two decades of negotiating construction disputes between owners, GCs, subcontractors, architects and engineers.

## Direct Quote (verbatim from source)
> "Disputes are a fact of life in the construction industry. As a practicing construction attorney for twenty years, both with a law firm and in-house at a national construction contractor, I have negotiated and helped resolve hundreds of construction disputes between owners, general contractors, subcontractors, architects and engineers. The [Arcadis report] issued by Arcadis reported that in North America in 2022 the average value of disputes on construction projects increased 42% to an all-time high, a staggering $42.8 million. The average time required to resolve construction disputes was 13.6 months."

## Lesson Extracted
Treat negotiation as the project's primary dispute-resolution forum — not as a soft alternative to arbitration. Quantify your BATNA (cost of counsel + 13.6 months of management time + US$42.8 M comparable dispute value) before sitting down. Otherwise the contractor's headline demand hides inside an unstated comparison to arbitration, and the negotiation collapses into positional bargaining.

## How to use this in a pre-arbitration playbook
Use as the framing for a pre-arbitration negotiation memo. The four-principle checklist ("separate the people… interests not positions… mutual-gain options… objective criteria") becomes a one-page template the project manager can run without counsel in the room. Pair with Case 001 (DAB is the cheapest BATNA) and Case 008 (write the without-prejudice offer down).
