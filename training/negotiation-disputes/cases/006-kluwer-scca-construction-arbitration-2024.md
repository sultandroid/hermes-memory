## Summary
Practitioner-oriented article on the procedural features that make the 2023 SCCA Rules attractive for KSA construction disputes — joinder under Article 12, multi-party/multi-contract handling, and Riyadh's emergence as the seat-of-arbitration of choice for Vision-2030 giga-projects. Frames the new Rules as the institutional backbone for what is now "set to become the norm" for KSA construction, and positions RIDW25 (Riyadh International Disputes Week 2025) as the inflection point.

## Lesson Extracted
Pre-arbitration settlement strategy in KSA construction must anticipate the joinder and multi-contract dynamics the SCCA Rules now embrace: any settlement should expressly reserve third-party rights, allocate contribution between joint-venture participants, and document what each package contractor has to release — before Article 12 joinder pulls them in.

## How to use this in a pre-arbitration playbook
Use this when drafting a multi-contract construction settlement in KSA. SCCA Article 12 joinder is the main reason a settlement that "looks final" between the GC and the Employer can be unwound by a subcontractor six months later — get them into the room (or on the deed of release) at the mediation stage, not the merits stage.
