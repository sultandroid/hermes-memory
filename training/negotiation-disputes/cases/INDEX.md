## Key Statistics

- **DRBF 2023: 85-98% of matters going to a Dispute Board do NOT escalate to arbitration or litigation.** Treat the DAB as live insurance, not as a precursor to arbitration.
- **SCCA caseload (Mayer Brown 2023): ~50% of SCCA-administered disputes are construction.** Construction is the single largest sector in Saudi arbitration.
- **Cohen Seglias Arcadis data (US$42.8M average dispute value, 13.6 months average duration).** Construction disputes are big and slow — strong incentives to settle early.
- **Dubai Court of Cassation Cases 31/2024 (3 Apr 2024) and 486/2024 (22 Oct 2024)** are the first onshore-UAE rulings to uphold the "without prejudice" principle in settlement negotiations. Strong precedent for the GCC.

## Open Gaps (honest reporting)

- **Saudi-specific "without prejudice" case law** is thin on the public web. No comparable Saudi-published ruling in this period was found. The Dubai cases (008) are the closest equivalents and the strongest precedent available in the region.
- **SCCA mediation settlement-rate %** — AI synthesis returned "70-80%" but no attributable primary citation exists. The index anchors the lesson to SCCA's verifiable caseload profile (~50% construction) instead.
