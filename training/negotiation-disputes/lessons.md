# Negotiation & Disputes — Top Lessons

> **Curated wisdom** extracted from articles in `cases/`. The 5-lesson extraction method is in `../READING_GUIDE.md`.

## From Article 001 (DRBF) — DAB Success Rates

1. **85-98% of matters going to a Dispute Board do NOT escalate to arbitration or litigation.**
   Treat the DAB/DRB as live insurance, not as a precursor to arbitration. **Strategic rule:** the goal of any tier-1 clause is to never reach the Notice of Dissatisfaction.

2. **Standing DAB beats ad hoc DAB in GCC/FIDIC work.**
   A standing DAB has continuity; an ad hoc DAB spends the first 3 months learning the project. Per Pinsent (Article 002), the choice between standing and ad hoc is the single most important drafting decision in a DAB clause.

## From Article 002 (Pinsent) — DAB Enforceability in Civil Law

3. **DAB success in civil-law jurisdictions (KSA, France, onshore UAE) depends on deliberate multi-tier drafting.**
   The Notice-of-Dissatisfaction window must be calibrated to the local court's approach to interim enforcement. A poorly drafted DAB clause is worse than no DAB clause.

## From Article 004 (Kabine) — When to Skip the DAB

4. **Some projects should explicitly skip the DAB tier.**
   Kabine (2024): small, fast projects with low complexity don't benefit from the DAB's overhead. The drafting decision: include DAB only when (a) project > 18 months, (b) value > $50M, OR (c) dispute risk profile is high. For everything else, go straight to arbitration.

## From Article 005 (Mayer Brown) — SCCA Construction Caseload

5. **~50% of SCCA-administered disputes are construction.**
   Construction is the single largest sector in Saudi arbitration. **Implication for clause drafting:** if you're a Saudi employer or contractor in any other sector, your case is *minority* — don't lift boilerplate arbitration language from construction cases without checking it applies to your industry.

## From Article 006 (Kluwer) — SCCA Rules for Construction

6. **The SCCA Arbitration Rules 2023 are FIDIC-compatible but have specific construction-relevant features.**
   Kluwer (Dec 2024): emergency arbitrator provisions are usable for urgent site issues (e.g. wrongful termination, payment stoppage). **Operational rule:** in the SCCA context, the emergency arbitrator can be invoked in parallel with the contract administration process, without prejudice to the main arbitration.

## From Article 007 (Cohen Seglias) — Fisher-Ury in Construction

7. **The four Fisher-Ury principles apply cleanly to construction claims:**
   - **Separate the people from the problem** — site managers and PMs often let personality drive the dispute
   - **Focus on interests, not positions** — the contractor wants cash-flow, the employer wants certainty; both can be served
   - **Invent options for mutual gain** — split the disputed scope, agree on a faster completion bonus, release a portion of retention
   - **Insist on objective criteria** — independent expert, third-party quantum assessor, FIDIC DAB

8. **BATNA (Best Alternative To Negotiated Agreement) is the leverage you should never reveal.**
   Cohen Seglias data: US$42.8M average dispute value, 13.6 months average duration. **Operational rule:** know your BATNA before the first negotiation meeting; never let the other side learn it.

## From Article 008 (Reed Smith) — "Without Prejudice" in Dubai

9. **Dubai Court of Cassation Cases 31/2024 (3 Apr 2024) and 486/2024 (22 Oct 2024) are the first onshore-UAE rulings to uphold the "without prejudice" principle.**
   Settlement offers marked "without prejudice" cannot be used as evidence of liability in subsequent proceedings. **Strategic rule:** always mark settlement communications "without prejudice" — in writing, in the subject line, and at the start of the message body.

10. **Calderbank offers (without-prejudice save as to costs) are now enforceable in the GCC.**
    Following the Dubai cases, a party that refuses a reasonable Calderbank offer and then fails to beat it at arbitration will be penalized on costs. **Operational rule:** make a Calderbank offer early, ideally in the first demand letter; if the other side refuses, document the refusal and proceed.

11. **Saudi-specific "without prejudice" case law is thin on the public web.**
    The Dubai cases (008) are the closest GCC equivalents. **Drafting consequence:** until Saudi courts rule directly, the Dubai cases are persuasive authority, not binding. Always pair "without prejudice" with an explicit reservation of rights.

## Cross-cutting Lesson

12. **The single highest-leverage negotiation tactic in construction disputes is contemporaneous documentation.**
    Cohen Seglias (Article 007), Pinsent (Article 002), DRBF (Article 001), and Kabine (Article 004) all converge on the same point: a party with better contemporaneous records wins both the negotiation and the arbitration. **Operational rule:** daily site diaries, weekly programme updates, monthly cost reports, and time-stamped communications are the cheapest form of dispute insurance.
