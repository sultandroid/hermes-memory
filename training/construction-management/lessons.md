# Construction Management — Top Lessons

## From Cases 001 + 003 (Mecca crane + KAFD scaffold collapses) — Temporary Works

1. **Vendor technical reports beat any subsequent forensic report.**
   Treat the vendor's incident-response team (Liebherr in Case 001) as a critical evidentiary asset on day one of any lift-related fatality. Their technical finding is the cleanest contemporaneous record.

2. **Two near-simultaneous Binladin fatalities (Mecca Sep 2015 + KAFD Oct 2015) shifted the regulator's frame to *organisational control failure*.**
   The post-2015 Saudi HSE enforcement frame is no longer "what went wrong on this lift" but "what is the contractor's organisational control regime for lift operations." This is the new enforcement template.

## From Case 002 (FIDIC SC 3.5) — Variation Determination

3. **FIDIC 2017 SC 3.5's 7-day deemed-acceptance mechanic is now the Site Manager's primary lever.**
   If you fail to respond to a Contractor's claim within 7 days, the claim is *deemed accepted*. **Operational rule:** on every variation request, calendar a 5-day reminder so you can never miss the 7-day window.

## From Case 004 (Drones) — Evidence

4. **Drones are now a primary contemporaneous record.**
   Operate a *preservation* protocol: capture the unaltered original (with EXIF metadata intact) alongside any processed version. Built-in smartphone enhancement is a built-in evidence-distortion risk (per Kluwer).

## From Case 005 (HSE UK L113) — Lift Planning

5. **HSE UK L113 (LOLER 1998) is the *de facto* lift-plan template for any Saudi/GCC site — adopt wholesale.**
   L113 + LOLER 1998 are not Saudi law, but every KSA lift-plan reviewer expects them. The standard sections (planning, supervision, examination, records) are universal.

## From Case 006 (2025 Saudi HSE reform) — Compliance

6. **2025 Saudi HSE reform converts "reasonable precautions" → "prescribed precautions".**
   "Reasonable" is a defence; "prescribed" is a checklist. Contractors who relied on "we took reasonable care" are now exposed to "you did not follow the prescribed precautions." **Operational rule:** every HSE procedure must reference the specific MoL standard, not general "best practice."

## From Case 007 (GAR 6th Ed) — Records

7. **The GAR 6th Ed five-record taxonomy is the canonical file structure for any construction dispute:**
   - **Programme** — the as-built + contemporaneous updates
   - **Progress** — daily/weekly site reports, photos
   - **Technical** — RFIs, design changes, MS, drawings
   - **Correspondence** — letters, emails, meeting minutes
   - **Commercial** — payment apps, VOs, cost reports

   Adopt this structure as standard from project start. Reorganising mid-dispute is a disaster.

## From Case 008 (Saudi Civil Transactions Law Arts. 463-467) — Statutory Duties

8. **Saudi 2023 Civil Transactions Law Arts. 463-467 codify *muqawala* contractor duties in primary legislation.**
   The muqawala chapter is now statutory, not just doctrinal. A breach of these articles is a breach of *primary law*, not just contract — which changes the remedies available.

## From Case 009 (IBA — FIDIC is procedural) — Daily Discipline

9. **FIDIC is procedural not substantive** (IBA). The submittal register, Notice log, DAB-eligibility tracker are the **daily discipline**. Substantive argument never saves a missed procedure.

## From Cases 011 + 012 (Multi-contractor sites) — Coordination

10. **The interface matrix + coordination log is the prime contractor's defence in cascading multi-party claims.**
    On multi-contractor sites, the Engineer's role is SC (Superintending Consultant) + CM (Contract Manager). Wrong Notice routing = wrong role = lost time-bar.

## Cross-cutting

11. **The contemporaneous record is the primary asset.** It is built daily or not at all. Substituting post-hoc reconstruction always loses to actual contemporaneous evidence.
