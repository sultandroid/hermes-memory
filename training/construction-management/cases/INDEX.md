## Three Key Patterns (from REPORT.md)

1. **The contemporaneous record is the primary asset** (Cases 004, 005, 007). Adopt the GAR 6th Ed five-folder file structure as standard.
2. **Notice discipline beats substantive argument** (Cases 002, 009, 011, 012). Run SC 3.5 / Cl. 20 / SC 2.3 with daily discipline.
3. **GCC arbitration now has a statutory peg** (Cases 006, 008, 010). Article 720 of the Saudi CC + Arts. 463-467 of the Civil Transactions Law + 2025 MoL Executive Regulations are the new doctrinal levers.

## Two Specific Pitfalls

- **Vendor technical reports beat any subsequent forensic report** (Case 001, Liebherr crane). Treat the vendor's incident-response team as a critical evidentiary asset on day one of any lift-related fatality.
- **Photography defaults distort evidence** (Case 004 citing Kluwer). Operate a *preservation* protocol that captures the unaltered original (with EXIF metadata intact) alongside any processed version.
