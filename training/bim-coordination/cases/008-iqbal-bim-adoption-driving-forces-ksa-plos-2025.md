## Summary
The paper classifies and prioritises Driving Forces (DFs) for BIM adoption in KSA using Fuzzy Delphi Methodology (FDM), Interpretive Structural Modeling (ISM) and MICMAC. The headline finding — that *"Reduced cycle time of the design process"* and *"Efficient construction planning and management"* are the main DFs to BIM adoption in KSA — is anchored to empirical survey data and contextualised within Vision 2030's USD 258.8 billion (2018) construction budget. The paper also situates BIM's 7D capability (7D = life-cycle / FM) as a Saudi priority, and reports the well-known industry statistic that "over 70 % of construction projects in (KSA) have exceeded their [budget/schedule]" — the entry-point for the BIM-coordination argument. The paper is the academic anchor for any Saudi-focused claim that BIM coordination is a *project-failure-mitigation* tool, not a discretionary "nice-to-have."

## Lesson Extracted
1. **The 70 % over-budget/schedule figure is claimant's ammunition**: A 2025 peer-reviewed PLoS ONE paper documents that "over 70 % of construction projects in KSA have exceeded their [budget/schedule]" — a robust foundation for any claimant's framing that BIM coordination was a project-failure-mitigation expectation, not an optional extra.
2. **"BIM can reduce unbudgeted costs by up to 40 %"** is the standard industry claim: the paper cites Hayek's established finding that "BIM can reduce unbudgeted costs by up to 40 %." A claimant's quantum will be cross-checked against this number — a contractor defending must therefore benchmark its own BIM process against this 40 % to demonstrate discharge.
3. **The top two drivers — reduced cycle time and efficient planning — are coordination outcomes**: Both ranking-first drivers are coordination outcomes of BIM, not technology outcomes. This re-frames the Saudi BIM mandate: the *coordination discipline* is the regulated deliverable, not the model files.
4. **The 7D BIM dimension makes the FM-handover case**: The paper situates 7D (life-cycle / FM) as part of BIM's evolution. Saudi Vision 2030 owners are now routinely issuing tenders that require 7D outputs — contractors whose BEP does not discharge into FM lose tender qualification regardless of design quality.
5. **Saudi specific institutional governance**: The paper cites the Saudi Ministry of Municipal and Rural Affairs as the source of the 70 % over-budget statistic. This is the institutional hook for any party relying on the KSA regulatory framework, rather than industry-association data, in a Saudi construction arbitration.

## How to use this in a BIM-coordination playbook
Use this paper as the peer-reviewed cross-check on the Saudi-context numbers. The FDM-ISM-MICMAC methodology is also a model for how to formally evidence BIM-coordination improvements in a SCCA / DIAC arbitration — i.e., applying multi-criteria decision methods to a defensible BIM-coordination claim.
