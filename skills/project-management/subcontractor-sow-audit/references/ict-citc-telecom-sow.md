## Purpose
Design, specify, and support approval of all telecom/data/fibre infrastructure for the Aseer Museum, compliant with **STC FTTH Design Guidelines Rev 3.0** and CITC standards. Coordinates with AV/IT contractor (Rawasin, Sub 03) and IT/Data Specialist (Salah Eldin).

## Scope
**Design**
- Telecom network architecture — structured cabling, fibre backbone, Wi-Fi, data outlets
- STC FTTH compliance — fibre termination, splitter cabinets, patch panels, ONT locations
- Separate security network — CCTV, access control (physically isolated per ER §3.4)
- BMS network — controllers, gateways, IP allocation
- Rack room design — servers, switches, patch panels, UPS, cooling, cable management
- DEMARC/MPOE coordination with STC

**Approvals**
- CITC registration evidence
- STC FTTH design compliance sign-off
- Coordinate with STC for fibre connection and handover

**Deliverables**
- Telecom single-line diagram
- Structured cabling schedule (copper + fibre)
- Rack elevation & patch panel layout
- Wi-Fi heat map & AP schedule
- FTTH design package (for STC approval)
- Test results & commissioning certificates
