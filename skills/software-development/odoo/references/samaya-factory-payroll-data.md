## Key facts
- **biotime_code** is the join key between workshop and Odoo (NOT odoo id). Mapping in
  `OT_SYSTEM/data/odoo_employees_biotime.csv`.
- **Factory = 4 departments**: Manufacturing (47), Warehouse (5), Transportation/قسم النقل (7), Printing (5) = **64 employees, ~162,233 SAR/month basic**.
- User classifies factory staff as: **عمالة ثابتة** / **مشرفين** / **إداريين**.
  - عمالة ثابتة = Labor/Carpenter/Painter/Welder/Driver/Machine Op/Technician/Applicator/Cleaner/عامل
  - مشرفين = Manager/Supervisor/Coordinator
  - إداريين = Engineer/Architecture/Warehouse keeper
- Total company payroll (from `employees_detailed.csv`): 528 emp = ~2,216,906 SAR/month basic.
- **Printing Production (مطبعة, 5 emp)** has NO باكدج/package anywhere — NOT in evaluation
  files either. Only basic salary. Do not fabricate a package.
- Full payroll MD: `reports/Payroll_Company_All.md` (regenerated by `scripts/build_company_payroll_md.py`).

## OneDrive deadlock workaround
Files under `Library/CloudStorage/OneDrive-SAMAYAINVESTMENT/Samaya/...` frequently throw
`OSError [Errno 11] Resource deadlock avoided` on read, even after `osascript quit OneDrive`.
Python `open(...,'rb')` retries and `cp`/`ditto` all fail the same way. Prefer the repo copies
in `samaya-workspace/DATA/`/`ARCHIVE/data/` — they are the durable source. Do not treat this as
a permanent tool failure; it's an environment quirk affecting OneDrive files only.
