# Claims & Arbitration — Saudi Commercial

> **Scope:** Saudi commercial arbitration cases, contract claims (مطالبات عقود), nullity petitions (دعاوى بطلان), and FIDIC-adjacent construction disputes. Reading these is the fastest way to internalize how Saudi courts and arbitration tribunals actually reason.
>
> **Source language:** Arabic primary, English translation provided by the user or extracted by me. Always cite the **case number** and **date** so we can look it up later.

## Reading Strategy (How to study a case)

When you give me a case PDF, I extract:

1. **Parties & counsel** — anonymized to roles unless the case is public
2. **Underlying contract** — type (lump-sum, unit-rate, D&B), key clauses invoked
3. **Dispute trigger** — what broke first (delay, variation, payment stop, termination?)
4. **Claim heads** — the 5–10 monetary categories claimed
5. **Tribunal reasoning** — what the tribunal accepted, what it rejected, on what legal basis
6. **Court ruling on nullity** — what the court upheld, what it struck (and why)
7. **Lessons** — vocabulary patterns, evidence standards, winning vs losing framings

## The Cardinal Rule (from the seed case)

> **Every riyal of claim must be tied to an actual incurred cost — not a financial abstraction.**

The court distinguishes:
- ✅ **Compensation for real damage + actual costs** (تعويض عن ضرر حقيقي وتكاليف فعلية) — this is the lifeline
- ❌ **Late-payment interest** (فوائد تأخير) — struck as ribā (ربا)
- ❌ **Lost profits** (أرباح فاتتة) — weak claim, often struck
- ❌ **Inflation / market rate increases** (تضخم) — weak claim, often struck
- ✅ **Bank-financing interest to bridge cash-flow during dispute** (فوائد التمويل) — defensible if framed as actual incurred cost
- ✅ **Contract balance unpaid** (رصيد العقد المستحق) — strong
- ✅ **Indirect admin costs from delay** (مصاريف إدارية غير مباشرة) — strong if documented
- ✅ **Extension of execution period** (تمديد مدة التنفيذ) — strong if tied to actual costs

## Common Vocabulary (Arabic ↔ English)

| Arabic | English | Legal weight |
|---|---|---|
| تعويض عن ضرر حقيقي | Compensation for real damage | Strong — survives nullity |
| تكاليف فعلية | Actual / out-of-pocket costs | Strong |
| فوائد التمويل البنكي | Bank-financing interest | Defensible if tied to bridge-financing |
| فوائد التأخير | Late-payment interest | Weak — ribā risk |
| أرباح فاتتة | Lost profits | Weak — hard to prove |
| مصاريف إدارية غير مباشرة | Indirect admin costs (overheads) | Strong if documented |
| تمديد مدة التنفيذ | Extension of execution period | Strong |
| رصيد العقد | Contract balance | Strong |
| خطاب ضمان | Performance/retention guarantee | Strong (release of) |
| أتعاب تحكيم إضافية | Additional arbitration fees | Capped — Art. "عاشراً" (10th) of typical Saudi arbitration deed limits to *new requests* |
| إرباك المشروع | Project disruption | Strong — the seed case turned on this concept |
| أوامر تغييرية | Variation orders | Strong if contract-compliant |
| رفض دعوى البطلان | Dismissal of nullity petition | The win |
| قبول دعوى البطلان جزئياً | Partial nullity | Common — usually only the "fees" paragraph gets struck |
| مخالفة المادة (X) | Violation of Article (X) | Specific ground of nullity |
| عيب في تشكيل هيئة التحكيم | Defect in tribunal formation | Ground of nullity (Art. 50) |
